Magic Kingdom Tap a land to filter. Check off rides as you go.

Lunch: Columbia Harbour House (salmon or shrimp). Liberty Square.

Characters: check My Disney Experience for times and spots, some have LL. Mickey is at Town Square Theater on Main St.

Lightning Lane: Multi Pass = pick 3 in advance, add another after you use one. Tier 1 rides are one per day. Seven Dwarfs and TRON are Single Pass, paid separately, max 2 a day.

Rider Switch: on any height-req ride, ask a cast member at the entrance. One of you rides, then you swap without re-queuing.

Baby Care Center: Main St, next to the Crystal Palace. Changing, nursing, quiet room.

Park map: Disney's live map (opens in the app if installed).

Closed: Liberty Square Riverboat and Tom Sawyer Island (gone for good), Carousel of Progress (refurb).
